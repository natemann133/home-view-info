Use and support

Updated October 27, 2026.

Who the app is for

Home view is privately installed software for its owner’s authorized home devices. This website is informational and does not offer public app accounts, subscriptions, camera access, or a software download.

Authorized devices

Use Home view only with devices and accounts you own or are permitted to access. Device manufacturers’ account terms, permissions, and service limits continue to apply.

Availability and limitations

Live viewing depends on the configured devices, home network, companion computer where required, and provider services. Connections can be interrupted or require renewed authorization. Home view is not an emergency monitoring service and should not be relied on as the sole way to confirm a door’s position or a person’s safety.

Support

For questions about this private installation, use the support address displayed on the Google authorization screen or contact the person who configured the app. For account, subscription, or hardware issues, use the relevant device manufacturer’s support.
