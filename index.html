Personal camera dashboard

Your cameras.
One quiet place.

Home view is a private Android dashboard for viewing cameras that its owner has configured and authorized.

It brings compatible live video into a single phone interface. Some cameras connect over the home network; others use a paired computer bridge and the camera provider’s services. Battery-camera video starts when the user presses Play.

This is an information page for the app’s authorization setup. It does not display camera footage, accept camera credentials, or provide access to a home.

Connecting Google Nest

Home view uses Google’s Device Access service to request live streams from an authorized Nest camera or doorbell. Google handles account sign-in and permission selection. Authorization can be revoked through the Google account and Nest partner-connection settings.

Built for personal use

The app is privately installed and configured. There is no public download, public camera directory, or service account registration here. Controls that are not integrated remain in the device manufacturer’s own app.
