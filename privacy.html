Data use and privacy

Private viewing.
Explicit connections.

Effective October 27, 2026. This page describes the privately configured Home view Android app and its companion computer bridge.

Google data the app accesses

With the account owner’s permission, Home view uses Google Nest Device Access to discover authorized devices and request live streams. This can include device identifiers, device and room names, supported streaming features, stream-session information, and live video and audio. Google may also require access to home information such as home names, room names, and ZIP code. Playback is muted in the current app.

The current connection is configured for live viewing, without camera-event or recording permissions. Home view uses this data to identify the selected device, establish playback, and renew or end stream sessions.

Where data is processed and stored

The phone and paired computer process live streams and temporary playback buffers. The current app does not intentionally record camera footage. OAuth tokens, pairing keys, camera connection details, and device metadata are saved locally on the configured phone or computer so the connection can be reused. The Android configuration is excluded from app backup and device transfer. Local diagnostic files can contain connection metadata and should be treated as private.

Google account passwords are entered only on Google’s own sign-in page. Home view receives authorization tokens rather than the Google password. Other camera services follow their own sign-in and streaming processes.

Sharing and third-party services

Home view does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. It sends the requests and stream traffic needed to the configured camera providers and between the owner’s paired devices. Google and other providers process that traffic under their own policies. This informational website receives no camera footage, camera credentials, or OAuth tokens from the app.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Local connection information remains until it is removed or the app is reset. To disconnect Google, revoke Home view’s authorization in the Google account or Nest partner-connection settings, stop the computer bridge, and remove its saved Google authorization/configuration files. Clearing Home view’s Android app storage removes its local phone configuration; it does not by itself remove the computer’s files or revoke Google authorization.

This website

These static pages have no analytics scripts, advertising, login forms, or camera integrations. The hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver the pages. If hosted on GitHub Pages, the GitHub privacy statement applies to that hosting.

Questions and support

Home view is maintained for personal use. Contact the app owner using the support address shown on its Google authorization screen. Do not include passwords, tokens, private connection links, or camera footage in a support request.

Google API Services

Home view’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
